Privacy Policy

Last Updated: June 10, 2024

Wise Medical Apps ("we", "us", or "our") operates the Medication Wizard mobile application. Your privacy is our absolute priority. We have designed this app with a privacy-first architecture.

1. Data Privacy & Health Information

We do not collect or store your personal health data on our servers. All medication names, schedules, dosages, and intake history are stored 100% locally on your device. We cannot see, sell, or share your medical records.

2. Third-Party Services

To provide a better experience, the App uses trusted third-party services that may collect information according to their own policies:

  • Google Play Services
  • AdMob (Advertising)
  • Firebase (Analytics)
  • Google Drive (Optional Cloud Backup)

3. Security

We value your trust. We use standard security protocols to ensure your locally stored data is safe, but please remember that no method of electronic storage is 100% secure.
