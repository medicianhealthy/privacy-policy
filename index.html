Privacy Policy

Last Updated: May 27, 2026

Wise Owl Studio ("we", "us", or "our") is committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how our mobile application, Medication Wizard (the "App"), handles information.

1. Data Privacy & Health Information

Your health and medication data are highly sensitive. We have designed the App with a "Privacy First" approach:

  • Local Storage: All medication names, dosages, schedules, and history are stored locally on your device using the Room Persistence Library and SharedPreferences. We do not have access to this data.
  • Cloud Backup: If you use the Premium "Cloud Backup" feature, your data is encrypted and stored in your private Google Drive account. We never see, collect, or store this data on our own servers.

2. Third-Party Service Providers

To provide a high-quality experience and maintain technical stability, the App uses third-party SDKs. These services may collect information used to identify your device:

  • Google Play Services: Core app functionality and authentication.
  • AdMob (Google): Displays advertisements. AdMob may collect your advertising ID to provide relevant ads.
  • Firebase Analytics: Helps us understand user behavior to improve App features.
  • Firebase Crashlytics: Tracks technical crashes to improve stability.
  • Google Play Billing: Processes Premium upgrades. All transactions are handled by Google; we do not store or access your credit card details.

3. System Permissions

The App requires the following permissions to function as intended:

  • Notifications: To send you timely medication reminders.
  • Exact Alarms: To ensure reminders fire at the precise minute scheduled.
  • Internet: To verify Premium status and load relevant content/ads.

4. Children’s Privacy

Our App does not address anyone under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personally identifiable information from children under 13. If you are a parent and aware that your child has provided us with personal data, please contact us immediately.

5. GDPR & CCPA Compliance

We respect your rights under the General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR) and the California Consumer Privacy Act (CCPA). Because we do not store your data on our servers, you exercise your rights (access, correction, deletion) directly through the App's interface or by uninstalling the App.
